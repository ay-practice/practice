What is free in this version?

Included at no usage cost: demo paper search/filter, local reading list, structured evidence-brief template, browser speech synthesis (voice depends on device), and slide-outline drafting/copying.

Not included yet: automatic live journal updates, genuine AI paper summaries, guideline-change monitoring, PPTX file generation, hosted audio, user accounts or cross-device sync. Those require additional integrations or code. We are deliberately not connecting paid APIs in this prototype, so colleagues cannot accidentally incur API charges.

Clinical note: verify all findings against the original paper and current guidelines before applying them to patient care. The demo records and generated templates are not clinical recommendations.
